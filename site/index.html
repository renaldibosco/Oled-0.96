OLED Lyrics Show

Plug the ESP32 into this computer with USB, then press the button (Chrome or Edge on a laptop).

  1. Pick the port named CP210x or CH340. If the list is empty, install that USB driver first.
  2. If it gets stuck on "Connecting", hold the BOOT button on the board.
  3. After it finishes, connect your phone to WiFi OLED-Lyrics (password 12345678) and open 192.168.4.1.

Wiring: VCC to 3V3, GND to GND, SCL to GPIO 22, SDA to GPIO 21.
